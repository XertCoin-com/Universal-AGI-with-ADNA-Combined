# Universal AGI with ADNA — Formal Proposal (2025)

## 0. Purpose
Formalize the ADNA + PoE protocol enabling decentralized AI societies that operate locally (including offline) and synchronize globally.

## 1. ADNA (Artificial DNA)
Canonical JSON schema tracking lineage, hyperparams, dataset commits, training logs, safety metrics, and signatures. Supports crossover/mutation with provenance and commit–reveal for ZK integrations.

## 2. Proof of Evolution (PoE)
Fitness S(G) combines accuracy, speed, energy, and cost. Proposer selection ∝ S(G) with dominance penalty and diversity boost. Fraud-challenge window for disputed claims.

## 3. AI Societies & Federation
Local subnets (micro-chains) evolve **offline**, periodically syncing to a global settlement layer. ADNA diffs merge via CRDT-like rules + governance-backed arbitration.

## 4. Storage & Sync
IPFS/Filecoin for permanence. Local watcher caches for low-latency reads. Gossip sync for model deltas; fairness windows to avoid head starts.

## 5. Governance
Stake-weighted quadratic voting + dynamic reputation index (contribution, accuracy, ethics). Electable DAC; recallable; policy modules for safety/risk.

## 6. Physical AI
Edge-first reference (Jetson-class) with MIG partitioning to host multiple agents simultaneously; sensor adapters and local policy engines.

## 7. Economics
Tokenized compute, licensing-as-a-service, reputation multipliers, and burn-based fee sinks.

## 8. Roadmap
P0: local devnet → P1: public testnet → P2: mainnet + cross-domain swarms.
